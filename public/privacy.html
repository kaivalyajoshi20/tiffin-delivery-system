TiffinFlow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Information we handle

TiffinFlow is a staff-only delivery management system. Authorized staff may enter customer names, phone/WhatsApp numbers, addresses, delivery schedules and operational notes. Delivery staff may submit delivery photographs and location coordinates as proof of collection and delivery.

How information is used

Information is used to plan routes, assign deliveries, verify delivery completion, maintain operational records and send delivery notifications when that feature is configured.

Photos and location

Proof photographs and optional GPS coordinates are stored for delivery verification and may be viewed by authorized administrators.

Security

Access is restricted by staff authentication. Production secrets are kept on the server and HTTPS is enforced in production. No system can guarantee absolute security, so access credentials must be kept confidential.

Browser storage and analytics

The application uses browser session storage for sign-in state. Optional analytics are loaded only after consent and only when an analytics measurement ID has been configured by the site operator.

Retention and requests

Operational records are retained according to the service operator's business and legal requirements. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the organization operating this TiffinFlow installation.
