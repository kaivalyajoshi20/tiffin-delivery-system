TiffinFlow is intended only for authorized staff of the organization operating the service. Do not share accounts, credentials or access links with unauthorized people.

Administrators are responsible for customer information, delivery schedules, driver assignments and notification settings entered into the system. Staff must record delivery events accurately.

Proof of delivery

Where required, delivery staff must capture the empty-tiffin and delivered-tiffin photographs and provide accurate location information. Photos must be limited to what is necessary for proof of service.

Availability

The service may be updated, maintained or temporarily unavailable. Route suggestions, GPS readings and notifications should be checked by staff and must not replace reasonable operational judgment.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to bypass authentication, access another user's account, upload malicious content, abuse the service or use customer information for unrelated purposes.
